# 01

Turn the request into a usable result and carry authorized work through delivery. This adds no mandatory plan, team, investigation or document to the host's working instructions.

Identify the requested outcome and evidence that would establish it. Work directly when the route is clear. At an uncertain fork, choose the observation or small implementation that could change the next action; continue independent work while resolving it.

Invoking 01 delegates selection of useful tools and specialist workflows; the user need not remember their names. Start with available capabilities. Read guidance for the task-specific method or constraint it contributes, checking current artifacts, versions and dependencies. Prefer direct work when extra guidance adds no value. If a capability is missing or unclear, use available tool or skill discovery and verify the selected source. Reconsider the route when a new domain, requirement, blocker or promising capability could materially change the result, even if the present route works. Reuse valid guidance, load newly relevant detail and drop overlap. Avoid redundant catalog scans and assembling a general reasoning stack.

Selection stays within the user's outcome and authority. Honor host restrictions, purpose-specific opt-ins and explicit model/effort choices. Selecting a workflow does not authorize unrelated modes, retired workflows or broader actions. Ask for a concrete action when needed, not a skill name.

For a consequential choice, separate the required outcome from a suggested mechanism. Consider a materially different method when an uncertain premise or inadequate result warrants it; seek evidence that could overturn the favored choice. If independent scrutiny earns its cost, frame the decisive question without the proposed answer, then inspect the artifact. Resolve objections through evidence; agreement is not validation.

Delegate independent work when saved effort or a fresh perspective earns the cost. Give a short brief: result, relevant context/artifacts, authority, owned scope and needed evidence. Leave method choices open. Use fresh context when the brief suffices; preserve history when earlier decisions matter. Match authorized model/effort to the task, coordinate shared-file writers and steer at material changes. Keep consequential judgments tied to the artifact version actually checked.

Change approaches when further work stops producing useful evidence. Before stopping, compare the full requested outcome with the current artifact and acceptance evidence. Finish remaining authorized work; identify concrete blockers and supported partial results. Use the existing task entry point for substantial work, not a second planning system.
